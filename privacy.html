MotorScope

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

MotorScope is a private competitor-research application used to analyze public marketplace listings and authorized eBay API data. This policy explains what information the application handles and how that information is used.

Information we handle

  • Public marketplace listing and seller information used for assortment, pricing, category, and sales-signal research.
  • OAuth authorization data when an eBay account holder expressly grants access to approved eBay APIs.
  • Technical records needed to operate the research application, such as collection timestamps and API response status.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate MotorScope, maintain authorized API access, perform private marketplace research, and produce analytical reports. MotorScope does not sell personal information.

Account authorization

MotorScope does not receive or store an eBay user's password. Authorization is handled by eBay. An account holder may revoke the application's access through the security settings of their eBay account.

Storage and security

Access credentials are treated as confidential and are limited to the application purpose. Reasonable safeguards are used to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of information.

Data retention and deletion

Authorization data is retained only while needed for the approved application purpose and should be revoked or deleted when no longer required. MotorScope honors applicable eBay marketplace account-deletion notifications and does not intentionally retain eBay buyer personal data for competitor research.

Third-party services

MotorScope may interact with eBay APIs and public eBay pages. Use of eBay services is also governed by eBay's own terms and privacy notices.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are published.
